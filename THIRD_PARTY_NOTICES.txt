Local changes are included in the snapshot and separately recorded in patches/.
The exported source includes upstream public test fixtures; it is not a dump of
any user's device memory or credentials.

Additional firmware dependencies are fetched by the upstream build definitions.
Their exact revisions and the Pico SDK revision used for the reference build are
recorded in firmware/build-manifest.json. Their original licenses continue to
apply. See the corresponding upstream repositories and source license files.

The desktop tool imports python-fido2 and uses the system GTK, PyGObject and
libadwaita runtime. Those dependencies retain their respective licenses.
